## [MINOR] ADR 0003 and spec §8.1 still say "first line" after normalize_message moved to first non-blank line
`docs/dev/adr/0003-fingerprint-is-a-frozen-public-contract.md`

The major-2 fix changed `normalize_message` to take the first non-blank physical line (fingerprint.py:33), but the two documents that define this frozen contract still describe the old rule: ADR 0003 line 26 ("`str(exc)`, first line, <= 200 chars") and docs/spec.md §8.1 step 3 ("`str(exc)` (first line, max 200 chars)"). The module docstring of fingerprint.py points at ADR 0003 as the stability contract, so the contract text and the implementation now disagree on exactly the step that was just changed. DECISIONS.md `p02-review_fix2/major2` argues the refinement, which makes the change legitimate, but a future reader checking the frozen algorithm against its ADR will find a mismatch and cannot tell which one is authoritative. Behaviour is correct and fully tested; this is documentation only, and it is cheap now and expensive after 0.1.0 freezes the wording.

**Fix:** Amend the one phrase in both places to "first non-blank line" (ADR 0003 line 26; docs/spec.md §8.1 step 3), citing `p02-review_fix2/major2`.
